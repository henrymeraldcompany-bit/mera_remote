Android Device Enrollment Guide

This comprehensive guide walks you through installing and pairing an Android smartphone or tablet with your MERA Administrator console.

1

Install the MERA Agent APK

Download mera-agent.apk from the Download Center onto your Android device. Once downloaded, tap the notification or open your Downloads folder and tap the APK to install. If prompted, toggle "Allow from this source" in your Android Settings.

2

Launch & Review Permissions

Open MERA Agent. You will be greeted by the transparency overview explaining that MERA operates an ongoing Foreground Service with persistent notifications. Tap "Begin Enrollment".

3

Generate One-Time Pairing Code

The screen generates a unique 6-digit pairing code (e.g. 482-195) and an animated QR code. The code is active for 5 minutes and single-use.

4

Administrator Scans / Claims

On your administrator device, launch MERA Admin, navigate to "Add Device", and scan the QR code or type the 6-digit OTP code.

5

Owner Grants Final Confirmation

The Android agent displays an interactive modal showing the Administrator's name and requesting authorization. Tap "Authorize & Enroll". Enrollment is complete and credentials are saved to the secure keystore.

6

Ongoing Status & Session Disconnect

A non-dismissible notification remains in your status bar: "MERA Agent Active". During any active remote session, a prominent "Stop Session" button allows you to sever the connection instantly.
